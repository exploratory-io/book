---
title: "AI"
---

AI features in Exploratory let you describe in your own words what you want to do. AI can wrangle data, retrieve data, interpret analytics results and draft analysis reports.

- [AI Prompt Function](/en/ai/ai-prompt-function) - Documentation for the AI Prompt Function is coming soon.
- [Wrangle Data with AI Prompts](/en/ai/ai-data-wrangling) - Describe the data wrangling you want in natural language, and AI suggests an R command that you can run as a step.
- [Get Data with AI Data](/en/ai/ai-data) - Describe the data you want in your own words, and AI assembles an R script that retrieves it, runs it, and builds the data frame.
- [Interpret Analytics Results with AI Summary](/en/ai/ai-summary) - AI summarizes the results of analytics such as K-Means Clustering, Factor Analysis, and Text Analysis in natural language.
- [Create Analysis Reports with the AI Note Editor](/en/ai/ai-note-editor) - Starting from a note that has only charts, AI automatically generates an analysis report that includes chart explanations.
