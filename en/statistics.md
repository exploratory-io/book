---
title: "Statistics"
---

Calculate correlations, distances, and similarities, reduce the number of dimensions, or run a statistical test.

- [Correlation](/en/stats/correlation) - Calculate the correlation among multiple columns or categories.
- [Distance](/en/stats/dist) - Calculate distances between pairs of variables or categories, which can also be used to find similarities.
- [Cosine Similarity](/en/stats/sim) - Calculate cosine similarity between pairs of categories.
- [SVD](/en/stats/svd) - Calculate coordinates by reducing dimensionality with SVD (Singular Value Decomposition).
- [Multi Dimensional Scaling](/en/stats/mds) - Represent the data with coordinates in fewer dimensions.
- [T-test](/en/stats/t-test) - Execute a t-test, which checks differences of means of variables.
- [F-test](/en/stats/f-test) - Execute an F-test, which checks the difference of variances between groups.
- [Chi Squared Test](/en/stats/chi-test) - Test whether the distribution of categories differs between two groups.
- [A/B Test (Bayesian)](/en/stats/exp_bayes_ab) - Run a Bayesian A/B test.
