---
title: "Share"
---

You can publish what you have built in Exploratory Desktop to Exploratory Server and share it with others. This section explains how to publish, set notifications, check version history and download published data.

- [Share Type](/en/share/share_type) - The kinds of content you can publish to Exploratory Server.
- [Sharing Chart/Analytics](/en/share/chart) - Share a chart or analytics by clicking the Publish button.
- [Sharing Data](/en/share/data) - Share data you have built or transformed at any step of the data wrangling.
- [How to Share Report (Note or Dashboard)](/en/share/note) - Publish a report (Note or Dashboard).
- [Notification](/en/share/notification) - Set notifications for team members when an insight is shared as a team.
- [Version History](/en/share/version-history) - Check when the insight was published from the Version History menu.
- [Restore Old Version](/en/share/restore) - Restore an old version of the insight from the Version History dialog.
- [CSV API](/en/share/csvapi) - Download data from the server for insights published with the download option enabled.
