---
title: "Product Features"
---

This section covers the features you use throughout your work in Exploratory: looking at data, building notes and dashboards, reusing steps, sharing your work and managing teams and projects.


## Look at your data

- [Summary View](/en/summary/summary) - View the summary information of the selected data frame.
- [Table View](/en/table) - View the details of the selected data frame, including the data in each column.
- [Row Filter](/en/table/row_filter) - Filter the rows to show by a condition on Summary View and Table View.
- [Column Filter](/en/table/column_filter) - Filter the columns to show by a condition on Summary View and Table View.

## Reports and dashboards

- [Dashboard](/en/markdown/markdown-dashboard) - Create a dashboard in Exploratory Desktop and share it with colleagues.
- [Note](/en/markdown/markdown-note) - Write a note in the Simple Markdown Editor and share it with colleagues.

## Work with steps and data

- [Reproducible Steps](/en/steps) - Operations on the steps on the right-hand side, such as copy, paste, cut, delete, move, disable, cache and export R script.
- [Branch feature](/en/branch) - Create a branch from the main data frame to explore a different sequence of steps.
- [Parameter](/en/parameter) - Make your data wrangling pipeline, charts, analytics, notes and dashboards more dynamic or interactive.
- [Export Data](/en/export) - Export data in various formats from the Summary / Table view.

## Share, schedule, teams and projects

- [Share](/en/share) - Publish your work to Exploratory Server and share it with others.
- [FAQ / Troubleshooting](/en/schedule) - Troubleshooting guide for scheduling data analysis.
- [Team](/en/team) - Manage teams.
- [Project](/en/project) - Import, export and search projects.
